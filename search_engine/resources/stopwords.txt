# Stopwords for search_engine/analyzer.py
# One lowercase word per line. Lines starting with # and blank lines are ignored.
# Removed words keep their position slot (so phrases stay honest).
# Checked before stemming, so the words here are unstemmed.
#
# Base: the Lucene / Elasticsearch default English list (33 words), which is
# small on purpose: BM25's IDF already gives frequent words almost no weight.
# Added: first-person and second-person pronouns, because bios are written
# in the first person ("I have 8 years...").
#
# it, or, as are here: their acronym forms (IT, OR, AS) are handled by
# acronym_stopwords.txt. no/not are removed too; negation is the query
# parser's job (-term, must_not), not the analyzer's.
# Do not add content words (full, senior, experience, ...) without checking
# document frequencies and NDCG first. After editing, rebuild the index.
